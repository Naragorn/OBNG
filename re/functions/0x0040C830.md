## Purpose

Draws one frame of the world into either the default target or a texture
manager target, in this order (each step **read** at the given address,
roles **derived** or **unverified** as marked in Claims):

1. Entry guard: byte [0x00B33395] = 1 (0x0040C868); cleared to 0 at the
   single exit (0x0040D136).
2. A pre-pass on the cell/grid side: `0x0049A100(camera, 0, 0)` on either
   `GetGridEntry(grid/2, grid/2)->+4` (exterior, [g_TES+0x34] == 0) or
   [g_TES+0x58] (interior with bit 1 of cell byte +0x24), then
   `[g_TES+0x54]->0x0049E880(camera, shadowScene)` with the shadow scene's
   byte +0x12C forced to 1 and restored (0x0040C86F-0x0040C8F1). Skipped for
   an interior without that bit.
3. When [0x00B3F940] != 0: 0x00701AD0() (only if its byte +0x1B0) and
   `0x00404D60(3)` (0x0040C8F7-0x0040C916).
4. Actor shadow maps: `0x004073D0` RenderActorShadows, unconditionally
   (0x0040C91B).
5. Hides the first-person node: `fpNode = player->0x00660110(1)`, remembers
   bit 0 of its word +0x18, sets it (0x0040C945-0x0040C95A).
6. Places two follower roots ([0x00B333D8], [0x00B333DC]) at the camera
   node's local translation (+0x54..+0x5C of the scene graph's first child)
   and runs `0x00707370(0.0f, 0)` on each (0x0040C95F-0x0040C9F0).
7. Selects the render target: with [0x00B42F3E] set, takes
   `textureManager->0x007C23C0(renderer, 4)` and, only when `msaaCopy` == 0
   (0x0040CA34 `JZ`) and the renderer's size differs from the target's,
   writes a scaled viewport into the camera (+0x110..+0x11C); with
   `msaaCopy` it goes to 0x0040CA36 and writes no viewport; then
   `0x007D7280(7, surface)`. Without it,
   `0x007D7280(7, arg->0x007D6FE0())` or `0x007D71C0(7, 0)` for a null arg.
   With [0x00B34FC0] >= 2 the surface comes from the renderer itself
   (vtable +0x7C) instead (0x0040C9F5-0x0040CC59).
8. Pass-state latches 0x00B42CD8/0x00B42CD9 and 0x00B42CDE/DF/E0
   (0x0040CC5C-0x0040CC91).
9. The main scene: `0x0070C0B0(camera, sceneGraph, cullingProcess, 0)`
   (0x0040CCD3), bracketed by `X->0x00544AD0()` / `X->0x00546120(1)` when
   [g_TES+0x5C] has a non-null +0x28 = X and its +0xDC is 2 or 3.
10. The first-person pass, behind gates (fpNode not hidden at entry,
    [0x00B42CDB] == 0): un-hides fpNode, `0x007C7050(fpNode, 0)`, swaps the
    renderer's accumulator (+0x08) for `0x007B7650()`'s object, brackets
    `0x0070C0B0(camera, fpNode, cullingProcess, 0)` with that object's
    vtable +0x4C(camera) / +0x50(), restores the accumulator
    (0x0040CD95-0x0040CECA). An optional `0x007AAA30(camera, surface)` on
    the same object runs before and after it (gate [0x00B42F3E] &&
    [0x00B42E84] && [0x00B42F48] >= 2).
11. Finish: with [0x00B42F3E], an optional device copy (vtable +0x88 of
    [renderer+0x280]), the image space step `0x007B48E0(renderer, target,
    arg)` and the camera viewport reset to {0, 1, 1, 0} (on every
    [0x00B42F3E] path, msaaCopy or not); without it, for a
    non-null arg, 0x007D72D0() and the optional device copy into the arg's
    surface (0x0040CED7-0x0040D043).
12. Releases the target (`textureManager->0x007C1EE0(target)`), signals
    [0x00B3F940] when it is non-null (InterlockedIncrement of +0x190,
    ReleaseSemaphore of +0x198; 0x0040D061 `JZ 0x0040D080` skips both),
    and, when [0x00B42CF4] is set, (re)creates a 0x20-byte object
    through 0x00496CB0 with the string "Render Passes" and 800 x 600, then
    drops [0x00B42CF4] (0x0040D045-0x0040D12C).

This function issues no draw call in its own instructions; the only device
call it makes itself is the vtable +0x88 call (step 11). The draws are in
its callees (derived; see claims C20-C22).
