## Purpose

Per tri-based geometry (NiTriShape or NiTriStrips) handed to the renderer, it:

1. returns at once when the renderer's byte +0x6F0 is set (0x007693E6/0x007693ED);
2. returns when the geometry data's virtual +0x50 returns a zero 16-bit value
   (0x00769400-0x0076940C; for NiGeometryData/NiTriShapeData/NiTriStripsData that slot is
   0x0051A9F0 = `MOV AX,[ECX+0x08]; RET`, objdump);
3. snapshots the geometry's world bound (+0x20, 16 bytes) and world transform (+0x64,
   13 dwords) onto its own stack (0x0076941D-0x00769449);
4. asks 0x00768890 whether to draw through the skin partitions ("use partitions",
   0x0076944D);
5. picks one of two renderer-owned objects (+0x8A4 or +0x8A8) from the data's
   consistency bits, the skin pointer and that answer, and hands it to 0x00778DB0, which
   makes sure the renderer data exists: each partition's +0x28 only when skin, usePartitions
   and skin+0x0C are all non-zero, else data+0x38 (C12; 0x0076946C-0x007694A2);
6. draws: through 0x00767520 (per skin partition) when "use partitions" is set, else
   through 0x007672F0 (whole geometry), both with pointers to the two stack snapshots
   (0x007694A7-0x007694D7).

It touches no global, no clock and no D3D object itself: every memory access is through
`this`, the argument or the stack (read: asm.s has no absolute memory operand; meta.json
`data_refs` is empty). The D3D device (+0x280) and the shader slots are reached only inside
the two draw callees.

## Callers and the place of shader slot 13

- **Who calls it.** NiTriShape's vtable 0x00A7ED5C slot 33 (+0x84) is 0x00717690 (shared by
  NiScreenElements 0x00A7E29C and NiScreenGeometry 0x00A80E34, same slot). It calls
  0x00722A10 with the renderer, then, when `renderer+0x200 == 1 || renderer+0x204 == 1`
  and `byte renderer+0x20C == 1`, calls `renderer->vfunc+0x160(this)` (objdump
  0x00717690-0x007176CA). NiTriStrips' vtable 0x00A7F27C slot 33 is 0x00719B60, the same
  shape calling `renderer->vfunc+0x164(this)` (objdump 0x00719B60-0x00719B9A). In the base
  NiRenderer vtable 0x00A7D6A4, slots 88-91 are 0x00982D85 (a shared stub, likely
  `_purecall`, unverified), and slots 86/87 (0x00701840 / 0x007018F0) also call +0x160 /
  +0x164, but NiDX9Renderer overrides 86/87 with 0x0076B310 (exe scan), so in the DX9
  renderer the only route found into 0x007693E0 is slot 33 of the geometry. Who calls the
  geometry's slot 33 per draw (the render walk 0x0077A1F0? a shader's pass loop?) is open.
- **Slot 13 is inside the callees, not before this function.** This function calls no
  shader at all (read: its only indirect call is the data's +0x50, 0x00769407).
  0x00767520 loads `[shader vtable]+0x34` at 0x00767684 and calls it at 0x0076769F with
  ECX = the shader (EBX, from the cast at 0x00767566 or renderer+0xA94 at 0x00767574), inside
  its per-partition loop (read, scene-pass export of 0x00767520). 0x007672F0 loads
  `[EAX+0x34]` at 0x007673BA and calls at 0x007673C4 inside its pass loop (objdump; that
  EAX is the shader's vtable is from the rea-all pseudocode, unverified).

## Open questions

- Who calls the geometry's slot 33 (0x00717690 / 0x00719B60) during the scene pass, and
  how often per frame: the render walk 0x0077A1F0, a shader's pass loop, or both? That is
  the link between this slice's walk and this function (the rest of the scene-pass
  slice; in progress).
- What is byte renderer+0x6F0 (device lost? a "skip geometry" mode?) and who sets it?
- What are the handler classes at +0x8A4 / +0x8A8 (their RTTI), and does their vtable +8
  reach the stream packer 0x00763130 via 0x00769030? That decides whether the 35 % packing
  cost OBVR measured enters here through 0x00778DB0.
- Since 0x00778DB0 builds only when the renderer data is null, where is the per-frame
  repacking of dynamic (skinned/volatile) geometry triggered: in the draw callees, in the
  shader's slots, or through the dirty bits of data+0x2E (0x00767520's pseudocode clears
  the low 12 bits of a +0x2E word, unverified which object)?
- Does the world transform / bound copy reach shader slot 13's last two arguments
  (worldTransform, worldBound per re/functions/0x007C9230.md) unchanged? Needs stack
  tracking in 0x00767520 and 0x007672F0.
- The gate in the callers (renderer+0x200, +0x204, +0x20C): what state is it (inside
  BeginFrame / a render target set)? A geometry submitted outside it never reaches here.
- 0x00722A10, called by both callers before the dispatch with the renderer: what does it
  do per draw?
