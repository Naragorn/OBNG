## What it does (read, instructions)

NiAVObject::Update 0x00707370: `[esp+4]` the time, `[esp+0xC]` the flag;
calls this->vtable+0x60 (UpdateDownwardPass(time, flag)); when +0x1C (the
parent) is set, calls parent->vtable+0x94 (UpdateUpwardPass) with no
arguments; RET 8.

NiNode::UpdateDownwardPass 0x0070A0D0:
1. If the flag's low byte is set: CALL 0x0047C930(time, 1) - the property
   list at +0x9C (an NiTList: node {next, prev, item}; each item with a
   controller at +0x0C gets its vtable+0x50(time)), then the controller
   chain at +0x0C (each vtable+0x54(time), next at +0x34). With both null
   the call does nothing (read, 0x0047C930 whole).
2. this->vtable+0x74 (UpdateWorldData).
3. FLDZ; `[this+0x2C]` (the bound radius) = 0.
4. For i in 0 .. `[this+0xB6]` (u16, the children NiTArray's first free
   entry): child = `[[this+0xB0] + 4*i]`; skip null. child->vtable+0x60(time,
   flag). Then FLDZ; FCOM child.radius; TEST AH,0x44; JNP skip - FCOM sets
   C3 alone for equal (AH & 0x44 = 0x40, odd parity: the skip is taken for
   a radius of 0.0), nothing or C0 for greater or less (even parity, not
   taken), and C3, C2 and C0 together for unordered (0x44, even parity: a
   NaN radius is not skipped). FCOMP own.radius; TEST AH,0x44; JP merge -
   taken for a non-zero (0x00) or NaN (0x44) own radius: CALL NiBound::Merge
   0x0072A6B0(&own.bound, &child.bound); for an own radius of 0.0 the
   child's 16 bytes at +0x20 are copied over the own bound. (The NaN cases
   were first read as skip and copy; the gate run of 2026-11-09 16:53 had an
   actor with a NaN child radius whose bound the original merged - 744 calls,
   and the reading corrected.)
5. RET 8.

NiAVObject::UpdateDownwardPass 0x00707580 (geometry, cameras, lights):
step 1 as above, then vtable+0x74, then vtable+0x78 (UpdateWorldBound).

NiAVObject::UpdateWorldData 0x0070C120: when +0x1C (parent) is null, the 13
dwords at +0x30 (the local NiTransform) are copied to +0x64 (the world);
else 0x0053D7A0(out, &local) with ECX = parent+0x64 composes them and the
13 dwords are copied. Then, when +0xA8 (the collision object) is set, a
tail jump to its vtable+0x50 (the collision object's own world update).

NiTransform compose 0x0053D7A0 (ECX = parent, `[esp+8]` = local, out): scale
= local.scale * parent.scale (FLD local; FMUL parent); 0x007100A0(out.rot,
local.rot) with ECX = parent.rot; 0x007101F0(v, local.pos) with ECX =
parent.rot; then per component out.pos = v * parent.scale + parent.pos
(the product first, stored to the frame, then the sum).

NiMatrix3 multiply 0x007100A0 (ECX = left, EDX = right, row-major 3x3):
each element is three products summed as the instructions pair them -
the first two products FADDP'd, the third added to that sum - with the
pairing differing per element (the exact grouping of all nine is in
src/engine/scene/SceneUpdateLogic.h MultiplyMatrix, element by element with
the instruction addresses). NiMatrix3 times point 0x007101F0 likewise
(element 0 sums rot[1]*v[1] + rot[0]*v[0] first).

NiNode::UpdateUpwardPass 0x00709EC0: this->vtable+0x78 (UpdateWorldBound:
the children's bounds merged as in step 4, the radius first set to 0),
then the parent's +0x94 (a tail jump); NiNode::UpdateWorldBound 0x0070A360
has the same skip/copy/merge decision as step 4 (read).

The exe's vtables with the two downward passes (read, a scan of .rdata):
eight NiNode kinds share 0x0070A0D0 / 0x0070C120 / 0x0070A360 (one has its
own UpdateWorldData 0x007416C0, one its own +0x64 0x004A0D50); eight
geometry kinds share 0x00707580 / 0x0070C120 / 0x00722AA0 (NiGeometry::
UpdateWorldBound, through the skin bound 0x0072BB30 when +0xB8 is set) and
two a BS variant 0x00864610; cameras, lights and particle systems have
their own +0x74; five classes override +0x60 (0x0047CE10, 0x007219C0,
0x0055D4C0 BSFaceGenNiNode - the actors' heads -, 0x00563EB0, 0x00724190).

## Precision (read, derived)

Every float operation runs on the x87 under the game's control word -
0x007F (round to nearest) or 0x0C7F (round toward zero), both at precision
control 24 (measured, knowledge/frame-clock.md) - so every product and sum
rounds to a float. A rebuild in SSE single arithmetic gives the same bits
when it keeps the grouping and copies the x87's rounding control into the
MXCSR (SceneUpdate.h RoundingFromX87): the oracle below shows 0 differences
under both words. Over- or underflow past the float's exponent inside a
three-term sum would differ (the x87 keeps the wider exponent between the
operations); game transforms do not reach it (derived; unverified in the
game beyond the shadow run).

## Why it costs (measured, knowledge/render-thread-outside-the-pass.md)

One ApplyAnimData call is 20 us for a plain NPC's 78 nodes, 50-60 us for
an armoured one's 300-325; 150-400 ns a node, of which the compose is
50 and the rest is the walk: a virtual call per child, the controller test's
two loads per node, the bound copies, the geometry bound's call. 1.3 ms a
frame on the Gold Road, 4 ms with 120 actors.

## The rebuild (src/engine/scene/SceneUpdateLogic.h, SceneUpdate.h; plugin/src/FastSceneUpdate.cpp)

The same calls on the same objects in the same order from a flat array
built once per skeleton root and checked against the tree's shape on every
call (Build, Matches): a forward pass does each object's controllers,
world transform (Compose, bit for bit) and - for a geometry - its own
UpdateWorldBound; a backward pass does each node's bound (radius 0, then
the children in index order by MergeStepFor, the merge through the
original NiBound::Merge). Objects whose vtable slots are not the two plain
kinds (the heads' BSFaceGenNiNode, particles, lights) run their original
UpdateDownwardPass, subtree and all, and their bound is merged like any
child's. The parent's upward pass follows, as the original's. A root that
is not a plain NiNode goes to the original.
