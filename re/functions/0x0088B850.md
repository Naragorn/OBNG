## Purpose

Casts one ray ("pick") into the Havok world that `this` (a `bhkWorld`) wraps,
on a per-frame budget. Before casting it counts the pick by the ray's collision
layer in five static counters and, when the frame's total or the path-pick
count is over its limit, gives a "no hit" result without casting. Otherwise it
asks `this` for its `hkWorld` (vfunc +0x58), casts the ray through one of three
Havok paths chosen by which result holder the caller supplied (all-hits
collector at +0x78, closest-hit collector at +0x74, or the record's own output
at +0x30) and whether a broad-phase cache at +0x70 is supplied, copies the
nearest hit into the record's output, draws the ray through the Havok debug
display [0x00BA7DA0], and returns whether it hit (**derived** from the control
flow below).

  result byte `[ESP+0x1B]` (0x0088BB0A) (**read**).
- Frame: `EBP` frame with `AND ESP,0xFFFFFFF0` (0x0088B853), an SEH frame

## The budget rule

- Counters (all dwords; names from the warning string 0x00A961E0 `"%d Havok
  picks (%d path, %d LOS, %d ViewCaster) used this frame. (Other type = %d)"`,
  whose arguments 0x0088C474-0x0088C491 are pushed in the order 0x00BA7924,
  0x00BA7928, 0x00BA792C, 0x00BA7930, 0x00BA7934) (**read (EXE)**):
  - 0x00BA7924 total picks, 0x00BA7928 path picks, 0x00BA792C LOS picks,
    0x00BA7930 ViewCaster picks, 0x00BA7934 last "other" layer (a store, not a
    count).
- Limits: total > [0x00B2E2F4] skips (0x0088B926); [0x00B2E2F4] is 0x28 = 40
  in the image (data.json) and is written only at 0x00441A6E from the setting
  at 0x00B05224, whose name pointer reads `"iMaxPicks:HAVOK"` and default value
  0x28 (**read (EXE)**; the only three encoded references to 0x00B2E2F4 are
  0x00441A70, 0x0088B922, 0x0088C46E). Path count > 0x1E (30) skips
  (0x0088B8C5/0x0088B8CD, a constant in the instruction) (**read**).
- So, with the default INI and the gate clear: the 41st counted pick of a
  frame and every counted pick after it is skipped; the 31st path pick is
  skipped even under the total; layer-0x14 picks are not counted but are
  skipped once the total is already over (**derived** from B3a-B5 and the
  values above). The count is taken before the check, so skipped picks keep
  raising the total (**derived**, 0x0088B8BB etc. precede 0x0088B91A).
- What the caller sees on a skip: `false`, [pick+0x50] = 0, [pick+0x44] =
  1.0f; pick+0x30..+0x40 and the collectors at +0x74/+0x78 are left as they
  were (**derived** from 0x0088B8CF-0x0088B8DD). This is the same as a cast
  that hit nothing for a caller that reads only +0x44/+0x50 and the return
  value, but not for one that reads a collector (**derived**).
- The increments are plain read-modify-write without a `LOCK` prefix
  (`83 05 ...`, `01 05 ...`, `A1`/`A3` pairs) (**read**). Concurrent picks can
  lose counts: an increment is lost only when two threads interleave inside one
  non-LOCKed read-modify-write on 0x00BA7924..0x00BA7930 (e.g.
  0x0088B8B6-0x0088B8C8) (**derived** from the missing `LOCK`). Whether
  0x0088B850 is ever entered from two threads at once is not shown by this
  body (**unverified**; settle by logging the thread id at entry in a timed
  run). The skip decision depends on how many picks ran earlier in the frame,
  so which picks are skipped depends on the order of all of them (**derived**
  from 0x0088B8CD/0x0088B926).
