## Purpose

The per-frame simulation step. It reads the clock once (`GetTickCount`,
0x0040D89F, **read**), hands the tick to 0x0047D170 on `TimeInfo`
0x00B33E90 (0x0040D8A5-0x0040D8AB, **read**; that 0x0047D170 is the
`TimeInfo` update which writes `frameSeconds` is **unverified** in this
export, see step 5), and then advances the subsystems in a fixed order,
each guarded by its own fresh `IsMenuMode` (0x00578F60) call. Six places read
the frame's seconds
(`TimeInfo.frameSeconds`, 0x00B33E9C = 0x00B33E90 + 0x0C); nothing in the
body writes it (**read**: the six `FLD`/`FMUL [0x00B33E9C]` at 0x0040D8B0,
0x0040DB8B, 0x0040DC8E, 0x0040DE62, 0x0040DE89, 0x0040DEAF; no store to
0x00B33E9C in asm.s).

### Order of work (answers the slice's first question)

"Reads dt" means it reads `TimeInfo.frameSeconds` (0x00B33E9C).

What "read" covers in this table: the call target, the receiver in ECX and
the values pushed before the call. Where a callee is cdecl (`ADD ESP,N`
right after it) its argument count is read too. Where it is not, which of
the pushed values a callee consumes is **derived** from stack balance at
the merge points, not read; step 22 has a split that stack balance cannot
decide and is marked **unverified** there (claim 19).

### Clock sources (answers the slice's second question)

- Inside this body `GetTickCount` is read exactly once, at 0x0040D89F,
  through the import slot 0x00A280D0 (**read**: the only reference to
  0x00A280D0 in asm.s and in meta.json `data_refs`).
- It feeds only 0x0047D170: the `PUSH EAX` at 0x0040D8A5 is the only use
  of the tick before the call at 0x0040D8AB clobbers EAX (**read**); that
  0x0047D170 consumes that dword as its argument is **derived** from stack
  balance (claim 2).
- Every dt consumer in the body runs after that call (**derived** from the
  six read addresses, all > 0x0040D8AB, and the absence of a back edge
  across 0x0040D8AB: the only backward jump is the actor loop at 0x0040D875).
- Whether a callee reads `GetTickCount`, `timeGetTime` or
  `QueryPerformanceCounter` on its own is **unverified**: the 106 callees
  are not in this export.
- Two other time-like inputs appear, neither a clock: the float scale
  [0x00B06530] that multiplies dt for 0x004424D0 (0x0040DE83-0x0040DE89),
  and the float [0x00B33A48] handed to 0x005732D0 in the one-shot block
  (0x0040DF3B) (**read**; their meaning is unverified).

## Calling convention and parameters

- `this` arrives in ECX and is kept in EBP (`MOV EBP,ECX` at 0x0040D807)
  (**read**).
- `this` is used as `[EBP+0x20]` (input, passed to
  `OSInputGlobals::PollAndUpdateInputState` 0x004046A0 at 0x0040D8C7/0x0040D8CD)
  and `[EBP+0x24]` (passed as ECX to 0x006AE860 at 0x0040DBB0), and itself
  as ECX to 0x0040D160 (0x0040DA60, 0x0040DEF3), 0x00407AE0 (0x0040DEFA) and
  0x0040D4D0 (0x0040DF52) (**read**).
- `this` is an `OSGlobals` (`*g_osGlobals`, 0x00B33398) (**derived**:
  xOBSE `GameOSDepend.h:355-356` lays out `OSGlobals` with `input` at +0x20
  and `sound` at +0x24, and OBVR `GameSound.cpp:15-16` reads the sound
  system from `*(0x00B33398)+0x24`; both offsets match the two reads here).
- No stack arguments (**derived**). With base = ESP after the four pushes,
  the frame is: saved registers base+0x00..+0x0F, locals base+0x10..+0x1F
  (`SUB ESP,0x10` at 0x0040D800), return address base+0x20, first stack
  argument base+0x24. EBP holds `this`, so there is no EBP-based frame
  access. The ESP-relative accesses fall in two groups:
  - into the locals: 0x0040DCB9-0x0040DCC7, 0x0040DCDC, 0x0040DCF5,
    0x0040DD07, 0x0040DD2C, 0x0040DE8F, 0x0040DE94, counted with the pushes
    before each;
  - into outgoing argument slots just made by `PUSH`/`SUB ESP`: `FSTP [ESP]`
    at 0x0040D8BF, 0x0040DB97, 0x0040DC9D, 0x0040DD0B, 0x0040DE6E,
    0x0040DE9E, 0x0040DEBE, 0x0040DF48 and `FSTP [ESP+0x4]` at 0x0040DD03.

  None of them reaches base+0x20 or above under any stack-balanced reading
  of the callees' `ret N`. Two places depend on a `ret N` that is not in
  this export and are checked both ways. 0x0040DCF5/0x0040DD07 land on
  base+0x18/+0x14 if 0x004C9CF0 ends in plain `ret` and on base+0x1C/+0x18
  if it ends in `ret 4`. 0x0040DE8F/0x0040DE94 land on base+0x10 if
  0x0067ACA0 pops its float. If it did not, they would land on base+0x0C,
  the saved-EBX slot, which compiled code does not write; still below
  base+0x20 either way.
- Stack cleanup: plain `RET` at 0x0040DF73, no `ret N` (**read**). The
  function is a `thiscall` with zero stack arguments, which for the caller
  is the same as any convention with ECX set (**derived**).
- Callee-saved EBX, EBP, ESI, EDI are pushed at 0x0040D803-0x0040D806 and
  popped at 0x0040DF6C-0x0040DF6F; EBX is held at 0 from 0x0040D81C and used
  as the zero for every compare and store (**read**).
- Return value: none; EAX at the `RET` is whatever the last call or
  `MOV` left (**read**: no `MOV EAX` before 0x0040DF73 on any path).
- Callers: none recorded (meta.json `callers` is empty). Who calls this is
  open.

functions that are mostly unmapped, 8 indirect calls (6 virtual at
