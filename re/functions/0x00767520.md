## Purpose

It draws one skinned geometry with a shader, pass by pass and, inside each pass, partition by
partition of the skin partition. It does not touch a clock or a global variable. In instruction
order:

1. **Shader choice**: it casts the geometry's +0xBC object to `NiD3DShaderInterface`. When the
   geometry is null or the cast fails, it falls back to the renderer's default shader at
   this+0xA94.
2. **Pipeline open**: shader slot 10 (+0x28). A non-zero result aborts the whole draw. Then
   shader slot 11 (+0x2C).
3. **Pass loop**: shader slot 18 (+0x48) opens the first pass, slot 19 (+0x4C) the next. Per pass:
   shader slot 12 (+0x30), then a copy of the world bound (a5) into this+0x658..+0x664, then the
   partition loop, then it clears the low 12 bits of the geometry data's word +0x2E.
4. **Partition loop** (0x2C-byte records): with the default shader, a partition with more bones
   than this+0x6D8 is skipped (the "print" is a bare `RET`). Otherwise: shader slot 13 (+0x34,
   0x007C9230 for four shader classes, reached through 0x0080B7A0 for HairShader: the per-draw
   setup that builds the bone palette when its gates hold), slot 15
   (+0x3C, returns a buffer record; null aborts the whole draw), slot 14 (+0x38), slot 1 of the
   object at `[[this+0x8AC]+0xFF0]`, then one `IDirect3DDevice9::DrawIndexedPrimitive` per strip
   (or per array) of the buffer record, then shader slot 16 (+0x40).
5. **Pipeline close**: shader slot 17 (+0x44), then `[this+0x8AC]` slot +0xFC with 0.

The only device call issued directly is DrawIndexedPrimitive (device slot 82, +0x148). Constant
uploads (SetVertexShaderConstantF), SetStreamSource and SetIndices are not in this body; when
they happen during this draw, they are inside the virtual callees (unverified which).

  [geometry+0xB8], &worldCopy, &boundCopy)`, after 0x00778DB0; otherwise it calls 0x007672F0.
  What 0x00768890's byte means (skinned or not) is unverified.
- **C16 derived** (from the access pattern): partition offsets +0x1C (vertex count), +0x20 (bone
  count), +0x22 (strip count), +0x24 (read by 0x007C9230), +0x28 (buffer data) and the stride 0x2C
  fit the Gamebryo `NiSkinPartition::Partition` layout as recalled (vtable, bones, weights, vertex
  map, bone palette, triangle list, strip lengths, then u16 vertices, triangles, bones, strips,
  bones per vertex, then the buffer data). The recall itself is **unverified** - no Gamebryo
  header was read.
- **C17 unverified**: proposed names of the shader slots, by the order they are called and their
  argument lists matching recalled Gamebryo `NiShader` methods: 10 PreProcessPipeline, 11
  UpdatePipeline, 12 SetupRenderingPass, 13 SetupTransformations, 14 SetupShaderPrograms, 15
  PrepareGeometryForRendering, 16 PostRender, 17 PostProcessPipeline, 18 FirstPass, 19 NextPass.
  Only the slot numbers, the order and the argument lists are read.
- **C18 unverified**: where the bone constants, the other vertex shader constants, the vertex
  streams and the index buffer reach the device during this draw. Candidates by position: slot 13
  (palette build, read in 0x007C9230.md), slot 15 (returns the buffer record; streams), slot 14
  (shader programs, constants), and the `[[this+0x8AC]+0xFF0]` slot 1 call right before the draws
  (a commit of pending state is a guess).

## Open questions

- What `[[this+0x8AC]+0xFF0]` slot 1 (0x007676F7) and `[this+0x8AC]` slot +0xFC (0x00767802) are;
  which class sits at renderer+0x8AC (render state?) - its vtable is not in this export. The
  +0xFC slot is the same one 0x007655F0 calls; one reading of that slot settles both.
- Which implementations of shader slots 14 and 15 the skinned shaders use (vtables of SkinShader
  etc. - read the COLs as 0x007C9230.md did) and whether SetVertexShaderConstantF /
  SetStreamSource / SetIndices are issued there.
- Whether slot 15 returns `partition.+0x28` itself (then C8's EDI difference is harmless) or a
  different record.
- The meaning of the low 12 bits of `data.+0x2E` cleared per pass, and of the top nibble 0x8000 the
  caller tests.
- The value of this+0x6D8 at run time (the bone limit without a shader) and whether the default
  shader this+0xA94 is ever used for skinned geometry in Oblivion.
- What decides 0x007693E0's choice (0x00768890's byte) between this function and 0x007672F0.
- Why OBVR's sampler puts 28 % inclusive here: the share split between slot 13 (palette, light
  constants), slots 14/15 (unread) and the DIPs (driver) is not measured.

## Layout requests

- `NiDX9Renderer` (0x00767520): +0x0C propertyState, +0x10 effectState, +0x280 device,
  +0x658 currentWorldBound (16 bytes), +0x6D8 maxBonesWithoutShader (u32), +0x8AC renderState
  object (with +0xFF0 sub-object), +0xA94 defaultShader. Shared with 0x007655F0 (+0x8AC, +0x8B0)
  and 0x007693E0 (+0x6F0, +0x8A0, +0x8A4, +0x8A8).
- `NiGeometry`: +0x20..+0x2C world bound, +0x64 world transform (13 dwords), +0xB4 data,
  +0xB8 skin instance, +0xBC shader (from 0x007693E0 and this function).
- `NiGeometryData`: +0x2E flags word (u16; top nibble a kind, low 12 bits cleared per pass).
- `NiSkinInstance`: +0x0C skinPartition (beside +0x08 skinData, +0x14 bones, +0x18 stamp, +0x28
  skinToWorld from 0x007655F0.md).
- `NiSkinPartition`: +0x08 partitionCount (u32), +0x0C partitions.
- `NiSkinPartition::Partition` (size 0x2C): +0x1C vertexCount u16, +0x20 boneCount u16,
  +0x22 stripCount u16, +0x24 (u16, read by 0x007C9230), +0x28 bufferData.
- Geometry buffer record (slot 15 result): +0x34 baseVertexIndex, +0x3C triangleCount,
  +0x44 arrayCount, +0x48 arrayLengths (u16*).
- `NiD3DShaderInterface` vtable: slots 10-19 at +0x28..+0x4C with the argument lists above.
