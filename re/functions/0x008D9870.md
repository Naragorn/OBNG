## Purpose

Fills a linear-cast collision input that lives on the caller's stack: copies the 16-byte path vector, stores the
path's 3D length (SQRTSS of x*x+y*y+z*z, w ignored) and a 4-byte value (the tolerance, proposal) into it. It is a
leaf: no branch, no call, no global, no heap, no Havok world access. It writes only the object it is called on.

## Calling convention and parameters

- `__thiscall`: `this` in ECX (read: 0x008D987F `MOVAPS [ECX+0x10],XMM0`; ECX used without being loaded).
- Arg 1 at [EBP+0x08]: pointer to a 16-byte, 16-byte-aligned vector (path) (read: 0x008D9879, 0x008D987C MOVAPS).
- Arg 2 at [EBP+0x0C]: a 32-bit value stored unchanged to this+0x08 (read: 0x008D98C2, 0x008D98C8).
- Callee cleans the stack: `RET 0x8` (read: 0x008D98CE `c2 08 00`).
- Frame: EBP frame, ESP aligned down to 16 (0x008D9873 `AND ESP,0xfffffff0`) and 0x20 bytes of locals (0x008D9876).
- Returns nothing used: EAX holds arg 2 at return (read: 0x008D98C2), a by-product.
- At 0x008ECC60's call (read: 0x008ECD19-0x008ECD2D): ECX = `ESP+0x58` after two pushes (the local cast input),
  arg 1 = `ESP+0x24` after one push (the path local), arg 2 = `[EDI+0x14]` (castInput+0x14).

## Open questions

- What 0x0090F200 is (a second phantom cast, e.g. a caching shape phantom, proposal) - not in this slice.
- Whether this+0x08 is the tolerance (C9): settled by reading what the dispatcher's cast functions read at
  input+0x08.
- Whether the w lane of the path matters to any reader of input+0x10 (F2).

Not worth a slice of its own: it is a leaf with no shared state. For the parallel-threads question it is clean
(C6); the shared-state risk of 0x008ECC60 sits in 0x008DE950 and the cast functions, not here.
