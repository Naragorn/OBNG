## Purpose

Packs one vertex stream (index arg6) of one geometry - or of one skin
partition of it - into that stream's vertex-buffer chip, and returns the
chip it used. In order (**read** unless marked):

1. Vertex count: the partition's word +0x1C, or the geometry's virtual slot
   +0x50 without a partition (0x0076FE35-0x0076FE4D). Count 0 returns arg5
   unchanged (0x0076FE56).
2. The declaration: `this->vt[+0x70](&d3dDecl, &streamCount)` (0x0076FE76).
   Its body 0x00770120 (objdump) calls `this->vt[+0x74]()` (0x00771150),
   stores that EAX to the first output and `[this+0x20]` to the second, and
   returns AL = (first output != 0). A zero AL returns 0.
3. The buffer-data record (partition+0x28, or geometry+0x38 without a
   partition, 0x0076FE88-0x0076FE91): 0x007780D0 stores the D3D declaration
   object into record+0x0C with COM reference counting and zeroes
   record+0x08; record+0x20[i] = the stream's stride (0x0076FEA9-0x0076FEB8);
   when the byte `[[this+0x08]+0x5C8]` is set, record+0x10 = `this->vt[+0x68]()`
   = the byte `[this+0x34]` (0x0076FEBB-0x0076FED1; slot body 0x0076FE10,
   objdump).
4. Destination: with arg7 != 0 the caller's pointer is the destination and
   nothing is locked (0x0076FEDF). With arg7 == 0 the chip (arg5) is
   validated - present, its buffer `[chip+0x08]` present, the geometry's
   word +0x2E nibble not 0x8000 (`(geometry+0x2E & 0xF000) != 0x8000`;
   "volatile" for 0x8000 is a proposed name, **unverified**), the buffer's
   `D3DVERTEXBUFFER_DESC.Format == 100` (D3DFMT_VERTEXDATA) and its `Size >=
   stride(i) * count` - and otherwise replaced through 0x00776C40 (release
   and allocate through the record's owner) and 0x00761AC0 (fetch
   record+0x24[i]), with the dirty mask forced to 0xFFF (0x0076FF66). Then
   0x00776C90 locks the chip's range and returns the staging pointer.
5. Per-entry loop over the stream's declaration entries (0x0076FFD3-0x00770083),
   filtered by the dirty mask (arg4) per selector group. PackEntry
   0x0076F590 is called for an entry the filter lets through only when
   softwareSkin (B+0x4C) is 0, or when its selector routes to case 6 (1, 2)
   or outside 0..14. With softwareSkin != 0 a selector 0/3/13/14 entry whose
   dirty bit is set is not packed; its destination is stored to
   B+0x28/0x24/0x1C/0x20 for the deform (0x00770003, 0x00770017,
   0x0077003F, 0x0077002B; B21/B23/B25/B27).
6. Software deform: only with a skin instance (arg2 != 0) and no partition
   (arg3 == 0) the position, normal and selector-13/14 entries are not
   packed but their destinations collected and handed to 0x0072AF20 with the
   geometry's positions and normals (0x00770089-0x007700B9).
7. Unlock: when this call locked, 0x00776D80 copies staging into the mapped
   buffer, leaves the manager's critical section and unlocks the D3D buffer
   (0x007700BE-0x007700D7).

Clock: the function reads and writes no clock and no global. Its only data
references are the two switch tables (meta.json); every other memory operand
is based on ESP, EBP (this), EBX, ESI, EDI, EAX, ECX or EDX holding object
pointers (**read**, all operands in asm.s). The callees 0x00776C90 and
0x00776D80 call three imports (0x00A2806C, 0x00A2808C, 0x00A28074; Ghidra
names them EnterCriticalSection, GetCurrentThreadId, LeaveCriticalSection -
names **unverified**) and no clock (objdump **read** of their bodies);
0x0072AF20 was not checked for clock access (**unverified**).

The jump table has seven used entries; data.json lists 32 dwords from
0x007700EC, of which entries 7 and later are the index table and the next
function's code (`cases` in data.json: the seven targets above) (**read**).

Test flows a rebuild needs: B1 both arms x B2 (count 0 / non-zero); B3
failure; B4 both arms; B5 in/out of range; B6 both; B7 caller buffer
(no lock, no unlock, returns arg5) against the lock path; the five
reallocation triggers B8-B12 each alone plus the keep path; B13 failure and
success (dirty becomes 0xFFF); softwareSkin for the four (skin, partition)
null combinations (B14/B15); B16 and B17 empty loops; every selector group
with its dirty bit set and clear, with softwareSkin 0 and 1 for the four
collectable selectors, selectors 1/2, 15 and -1; B30 both; B31/B32 unlock
success and failure.

## Claims

C1. **read** - Contract: `__thiscall`, ECX = the declaration, seven stack
arguments `(geometry, skin, partition, dirty, chip, streamIndex,
lockedDest)`, `RET 0x1C` (0x0076FE85, 0x007700E8). The argument roles of
geometry, partition, dirty, chip, streamIndex and lockedDest follow from the
accesses in the parameter table; "skin" is the packer's name
(re/functions/0x00763130.md C13) and is only null-tested here and handed on
(PackEntry arg3, ECX of 0x0072AF20).

C2. **read** - The loop runs over the entries of one stream: j = 0 ..
[this+0x1C]-1 (0x0076FFC4, 0x0077007C), entry = `[stream+0x08] + j*0x1C`
with stream = `[this+0x24] + i*0x10` and i = arg6 (0x0076FFA8-0x0076FFE2,
EDI += 0x1C at 0x00770079). The bound is re-read every iteration.

C3. **read** - The filter is the dirty mask arg4 tested per selector group:
selector 0 against bit 0x1, selectors 3/13/14 against 0x2, selector 4
against 0x4, selectors 5..12 against 0x8 (0x0076FFF7-0x00770053);
selectors 1 and 2 and every selector outside 0..14 are packed without a
test (index 6 and B18 -> 0x00770055). **derived** (C3 with
re/functions/0x00763130.md C13, where the packer pushes its dirty mask as
the fourth argument): the two seed readings are the same thing - the
"argument-4 bits 1/2/4/8 against selector groups" ARE the geometry's dirty
bits (or 0xFFF), so the packer's "per stream with the dirty mask" and
PackEntry's "caller filters by argument-4 bits" agree. Only the low byte of
arg4 is tested (all six tests are `TEST byte`); bits 0x10 and up never
reach a decision in this body.

C4. **read** - Per entry passed through and not collected for the deform
(softwareSkin == 0, or selectors 1/2 and outside 0..14; B21/B23/B25/B27
and C9 for the collected ones), PackEntry 0x0076F590 is called once
with `(count, geometry, skin, partition, entry, stream.stride,
base + entry.offset)` (pushes 0x00770059-0x00770069; base = B+0x64 added at
0x0076FFDE), where count is the partition's or geometry's vertex count from
B1. Its return value is not tested: EAX is overwritten at 0x00770072
(**read**), so a failed entry does not stop the loop or change the result.

C5. **read** - Lock and unlock are per stream, not per entry: one
`0x00776C90` call at 0x0076FF83 before the loop and one `0x00776D80` call at
0x007700D0 after the loop and after the deform; there is no call to either
inside the loop (0x0076FFD3-0x00770083 calls only 0x0076F590). **derived**
(C5, B7, B2, B3, B13): a call to this function performs at most one
lock/unlock pair, and none when arg7 != 0, the count is 0, slot +0x70 fails
or the reallocation fails.

C6. **read** (objdump) - The staging copy: the lock wrapper returns the
manager's staging block `[mgr+0x40]` (0x00776D28), not the mapped pointer,
which it keeps at `[mgr+0x48]` (0x00776CED). **derived** (C4, B7 and the
store of EAX to B+0x64 at 0x0076FF8C): only on the lock path (arg7 == 0) is
every PackEntry and deform destination an address inside the staging block;
with arg7 != 0 every destination is entry.offset + the caller's pointer
(0x0076FEDF -> 0x0076FF92) and no staging block is involved. The unlock
copies staging -> mapped with `CALL 0x009812C0` at 0x00776D8F (memcpy by
Ghidra's name and by knowledge/skinned-vertex-streams.md, name
**unverified** here). **Correction for OBVR and the knowledge file:** the
instruction at 0x00776D8F is that `CALL`, not a `rep movsd`; the `rep
movsd` (`F3 A5`) is inside 0x009812C0 at 0x0098131A (**read**, objdump), so
the knowledge file has the wrong address, not the wrong mechanism. The
order inside one lock-path pack is (**read**, objdump of 0x00776C90 and
0x00776D80): lock (D3D Lock `CALL [vt+0x2C]` at 0x00776CBC, then
EnterCriticalSection at 0x00776CD3 only on success, optional read-back) ->
all PackEntry calls -> deform -> unlock (staging copy, leave critical
section, D3D Unlock).

C7. **read** (objdump) - When the chip's lock flags have neither 0x1000
(NOOVERWRITE) nor 0x2000 (DISCARD), the lock wrapper also copies the
mapped buffer into the staging block before packing (0x00776D0E-0x00776D20).
**derived**: on such a lock every packed stream crosses memory three times
(read-back, pack, staging copy). Which flags the chips carry in play is
**unverified** (knowledge/skinned-vertex-streams.md says the ring locks with
0x1000 or 0x2000, which would skip the read-back).

C8. **read** - The chip is replaced (0x00776C40, then 0x00761AC0) when it is
null, has no buffer, the geometry's consistency nibble is 0x8000, the
buffer's Format is not 100 or its Size is smaller than
`this->vt[+0x60](i) * count` (B8-B12). After a replacement the dirty mask is
0xFFF (0x0076FF66), so every selector group is packed. **read** (B10,
0x0076FF13): in the lock path (arg7 == 0) a geometry whose word +0x2E has
`& 0xF000 == 0x8000` always goes to the reallocation 0x00776C40, which runs
`owner->vt[+0x1C](record, i)` then `owner->vt[+0x18](record, i)` (objdump),
and then re-fetches record+0x24[i] (0x00761AC0). **derived**: every such
lock-path call requests a fresh chip; the request can fail (return 0 at
0x0076FF54). Whether the owner's slots hand back a new allocation, a pooled
chip or the same chip is **unverified** (a read of the owner's slot bodies
+0x18/+0x1C, or a runtime compare of the chip before and after, settles
it). Calling the 0x8000 nibble "volatile" rests on recalled Gamebryo enum
values and is **unverified** in this export. What the owner is (the ring
manager 0x007829A0?) is **unverified**.

C9. **read** - The software deform runs only when skin != 0 and partition
== 0 (B14/B15, B30). Then positions (selector 0), normals (3) and the two
trailing arrays (14, 13) are not packed by PackEntry; their destinations
are collected and passed with the geometry's positions [+0x1C], normals
[+0x20] and vertex count word [+0x08] to `skin->0x0072AF20(positions,
normals, count, dest0, dest3, dest14, dest13, stride)` (pushes
0x0077009B-0x007700B8, ECX = skin at 0x007700B3). A destination whose
dirty bit was clear, or whose selector is not in the declaration, stays 0
(zeroed at 0x0076FFAE-0x0076FFBA). **derived** (C9, re/functions/0x00763130.md
C13 passes the partition non-null as the third argument when it has one):
for a skinned body drawn through partitions this function does no CPU
deform; positions and normals are packed as they are, with the partition's
blend weights and indices (selectors 1, 2) - consistent with GPU skinning.
Whether the packer ever passes a null partition with a skin is
**unverified**.

C10. **read** - Slot +0x70 (0x00770120, objdump) returns the declaration's
D3D object via `this->vt[+0x74]()` and `[this+0x20]`; a null D3D object
makes this function return 0 before any lock (B3). The second output (B+0x2C)
is never read here (**read** by absence).

C11. **read** - The record update happens before the destination is chosen
and on both the lock and the caller-buffer path: record+0x0C = the D3D
declaration (0x0076FE9B), record+0x20[i] = stride (0x0076FEB8, only when
i < record+0x1C), record+0x10 = decl byte +0x34 when the byte
`[[this+8]+0x5C8]` is set (0x0076FED1).

C12. **read** - Return value: the chip used (arg5 or the re-fetched one) on
success, 0 on failure, and arg5 unchanged when the count is 0 (0x0076FE56).
**derived**: with count 0 and a null chip the caller sees 0, the same value
as a failure; the packer then falls back or fails
(re/functions/0x00763130.md B20/B21).

C13. **read** - The lock wrapper's result is not tested: 0x0076FF88-0x0076FF90
store EAX to B+0x64 without a TEST. **derived** (with objdump 0x00776CC2-
0x00776CC8: a failed D3D Lock returns 0 without entering the critical
section): a failed Lock passes a null base to PackEntry and the deform, so
their destinations are `entry.offset + 0`, a near-null address; the first
write would most likely fault before the unlock (**unverified**, no fault
was observed). If it survived - only when no entry writes - the unlock
0x00776D80 would memcpy, decrement +0xFC and leave a critical section that
was never entered. Whether Lock ever fails in play is **unverified**.

C14. **read** - The function reads and writes no clock and no global; its
only data references are the switch tables 0x00770108 and 0x007700EC
(meta.json; all memory operands in asm.s).

C15. **derived** (C5, re/functions/0x00763130.md C12/C13) - Per pack of a
skinned body through the packer: one lock/unlock pair per stream the packer
loops over (decl+0x20 streams) per partition, when the dirty mask has a bit
other than 0x10; plus the packer's own index-buffer path (0x007781F0, not
read here). The 135 unlocks a pass with five people (knowledge) cannot be
split into streams x partitions from instructions alone: the stream count
per declaration and partitions per body are run-time values
(**unverified**; a runtime count of calls per stream index and partition
would settle it).

C16. **read** - pseudo.c agrees with the instructions on the branch
structure, the selector groups, the argument order of every call and the
return values; it omits the ECX (`this`) of 0x007780D0 (record),
0x00761AC0 (record), 0x00776C90 and 0x00776D80 (buffer manager) and
0x0072AF20 (skin), and shows 0x00776C40 with the two pushed arguments only,
which is right for that callee (it ignores ECX, objdump). Ghidra's
`param_N` is shifted by one against this file's argument numbers
(param_1 = this).

C17. **read** (objdump) - What the four callees asked about are:
0x0072AF20 is an eight-argument thiscall on the skin instance (`RET 0x20`)
called only on the software-skin path (C9) - the CPU deform (name
**unverified**); 0x00761AC0 is `record.GetChip(i)` (bounded array read
record+0x24[i]); 0x00776C40 is `ReallocateChip(record, i)` (owner release

record+0x08.

## Open questions

1. What are the record owner's slots +0x18 (allocate) and +0x1C (release)?
   If the owner is the dynamic-buffer ring 0x007829A0, every geometry with
   nibble 0x8000 (C8) requests a new ring slice per lock-path pack - which
   decides whether the
   ring's NOOVERWRITE/DISCARD flags reach the chip's +0x10 and so whether
   the read-back in C7 ever runs.
2. Which lock flags do chips carry in play (chip+0x10)? A probe on
   0x00776C90's fourth argument settles C7.
3. Does any caller pass arg7 != 0 (a pre-locked destination)? The packer
   passes 0 (re/functions/0x00763130.md C13); the other callers named in
   knowledge/skinned-vertex-streams.md (0x00771790, 0x00773330) were not
   checked at instruction level.
4. Does the software-deform path (skin != 0, partition == 0) run in play?
   A counter on the call at 0x007700B9 settles it, and with it whether the
   CPU transforms any vertices (knowledge/skinned-vertex-streams.md, last
   section).
5. What is `[this+0x08]` and its byte +0x5C8 (renderer? a "store flag in
   record" switch), and what does decl byte +0x34 mean?
6. Is a failed D3D Lock (C13) possible under DXVK? If yes, this is a latent
   crash in the engine, not in OBNG.
7. The split of the 135 unlocks a pass (C15): a per-call counter on
   0x0076FE20's lock path and on 0x007781F0 would settle it.

Callees worth their own slice: 0x0072AF20 (the CPU deform, large, only if
open question 4 says it runs); 0x00771150 (decl slot +0x74, rebuilds the D3D
vertex declaration when the byte +0x28 is set - objdump of its head only);
the lock/unlock pair 0x00776C90 / 0x00776D80 (small, the staging copy, a
candidate for writing straight into the mapped pointer); 0x00776C40 with the
record owner's slots +0x18 / +0x1C.

`StreamRecord` (0x10 bytes): +0x08 `DeclEntry* entries`, +0x0C `UInt32 stride`;
+0x00..+0x07 not touched here.

`DeclEntry` (0x1C bytes): +0x04 `UInt32 offset`, +0x08 `Int32 selector`,
+0x0C `UInt32 destType` (PackEntry); the rest not touched here.

Buffer-data record (partition+0x28 / geometry+0x38): +0x04 owner, +0x08
(zeroed), +0x0C D3D declaration (COM), +0x10 byte flag, +0x1C streamCount,
+0x20 `UInt32* strides`, +0x24 `Chip** chips` - extends the record in
re/functions/0x00763130.md.

Chip: +0x08 `IDirect3DVertexBuffer9*`, +0x0C lock offset, +0x10 lock flags,
+0x14 lock size.

Buffer manager (`[decl+0x0C]`): +0x40 staging block, +0x44 staging
capacity, +0x48 mapped pointer, +0x4C locked size, +0x80 critical section,
+0xF8 owning thread id, +0xFC lock depth (0x00776C90 / 0x00776D80, objdump).

Geometry data: +0x08 u16 vertexCount, +0x1C positions, +0x20 normals,
+0x2E u16 consistency/dirty, +0x38 record (no-partition case).
Partition: +0x1C u16 vertexCount, +0x28 record.
