## Purpose

Takes one camera's location, three axes, frustum and viewport (as handed in by 0x00701970) and,
unless the renderer's byte +0x6F0 is set, in this order:

1. stores the camera location into the global vector **0x00B3F92C/30/34** (0x00762657..0x00762677);
   this is the vector the world-matrix packer 0x00761AE0 subtracts from every world translation into
   renderer+0x940 (pseudocode, rea-all), the bone-palette builder 0x007655F0 subtracts from every bone
   translation (re/functions/0x007655F0.md) and SetupTransformations 0x007C9230 uses as the eye
   (re/functions/0x007C9230.md C14) - i.e. the renderer draws **camera-relative**;
2. builds the **view matrix at renderer+0x980** (D3D row-vector form): columns are right (arg4),
   up (arg3), direction (arg2), translation row `-(axis . T)` where T is the global vector
   **0x00B3F9A8/AC/B0** - T is not taken from arg1 (read); whether it ever holds the camera
   location at run time is unverified (C4);
3. builds the **inverse view at renderer+0xA00**: rows (right,0), (up,0), (dir,0), (T,1);
4. `device->SetTransform(D3DTS_VIEW, renderer+0x980)`;
5. copies right and up into renderer +0x628/+0x634 and +0x640/+0x64C; stores near at +0x678 and
   far-near at +0x67C;
6. builds the **projection at renderer+0x9C0** from the frustum (perspective or orthographic by the
   frustum's byte +0x18, X mirrored or not by `[[this+0x8AC]]+0x48()`), then
   `device->SetTransform(D3DTS_PROJECTION, renderer+0x9C0)`;
7. asks `[this+0x87C]` for a width (+0x4C(0)) and height (+0x50(0)), builds a D3DVIEWPORT9 on the
   stack from the camera's port rectangle and calls `device->SetViewport` - the viewport is **not**
   kept in a renderer field;
8. calls `[[this+0x8AC]]+0x44(near, far)`.

  and drops Width, Height, MinZ, MaxZ of the viewport; (e) its final call omits ECX = [this+0x8AC]
  and passes `param_3[4], param_3[5]`, which are frustum near/far (arg5) in asm; (f) it uses
  `pfVar6` (r-l, cast to a pointer, pseudo.c line 81) as the port for the viewport instead of arg6
  (asm loads arg6 at 0x00762ABC); (g) it discards the +0x4C width result (pseudo.c line 145) where
  asm stores it as W (0x00762A9D); (h) it writes `ROUND` (lines 151-152) where asm truncates via
  RC = 11b (C7); (i) it omits ECX = [this+0x87C] on the +0x4C/+0x50 calls (lines 145-146). The
  projection formulas and the B2/B3/B6 structure in pseudo.c agree with asm.s.
- **C14 derived (stereo contract)**: per eye, the eye-dependent state this function produces is
  (a) device: VIEW transform, PROJECTION transform, viewport; (b) renderer fields: +0x980, +0x9C0,
  +0xA00, +0x628/+0x634, +0x640/+0x64C (written from arg4/arg3 at 0x00762815..0x00762861, read),
  +0x678/+0x67C; (c) the global 0x00B3F92C; (d) any state kept by K7 `[[this+0x8AC]]+0x44(near,
  far)` at 0x00762BC9 and by K2 - unaccounted for, unverified (their bodies settle it). Because the world
  matrices (+0x940 via 0x00761AE0) and the bone palettes (0x007655F0) subtract 0x00B3F92C at pack
  time, changing 0x00B3F92C between two draws of already-packed geometry leaves those packed
  translations relative to the old origin. With 0x00B3F92C kept at one origin for both eyes, an
  eye's offset can be carried in the view's translation row alone (`_4k = -(axis_k . e)` with e the
  eye's position relative to that origin) - except where 0x007C9230 C14 rebuilds the view's
  translation from 0x00B3F92C (shadow-light clip planes, passes 0x177..0x17A), which would drop it.
  Not measured; the design consequence follows from C2, C4 and the cited files.
- **C15 derived (cached products)**: this function's own instructions write no V x P product, no
  dirty flag and no frame stamp; the effects of the callees it does not follow
  (`[[this+0x8AC]]+0x48`, `[[this+0x8AC]]+0x44(near, far)` at 0x00762BC9, `[[this+0x87C]]+0x4C/+0x50`
  and the D3D calls, all outside the export) are unverified. The products are formed where they are consumed (re/functions/0x009A56D0.md C4: the
  constant map multiplies +0x940/+0x980/+0x9C0 per request; 0x007C9230 C14 inverts V x P for clip
  planes). Whether any consumer caches a product across draws is unverified here (0x007C9230's
  plane cache at light+0x1B4/+0x214 is one such cache: re/functions/0x007C9230.md C15).
