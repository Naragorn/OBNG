## Purpose

Walks the process manager's high-process actor list (the inline list head at this+0x68) once from head to
tail, and for every actor that passes the flag and process-level filters: measures its distance to the
player, runs its update through actor vfunc +0x1C0 (or, when 0x005F1330 says so, a process-side branch
instead), runs three per-actor steps when the player's +0x590 is positive, drops the cursor when the actor
left the high level, feeds a staggered time to vfunc +0x1D4 and adds near actors to the manager's list
+0x60 (the shadow step's list), and then updates the actors listed in the actor's extra data of type 0x23
that are not high themselves. Once at the end it calls 0x00677500 with the frame seconds.

(derived from the instructions below; the meaning of the vfuncs and of extra type 0x23 is unverified)

- Arg 1 (ESP0+4): a float time. Read only at 0x00677FF4 (`FLD [ESP+0x38]` with 3 pushes + EBP + 0x24
  frame = ESP0+4) as the argument of vfunc +0x1C0, and at 0x00678257 for the extra-list actors (read;

- Arg 2 (ESP0+8): never read in the body (derived: no access at ESP0+8 at any stack depth in asm.s).
  pseudo.c shows only one float parameter; the instructions show two stack dwords cleaned (pseudo.c
  disagrees with RET 8 - the instructions win).
- this = the ActorProcessManager 0x00B3BD00 when called from 0x00678510 (derived: the high list at +0x68
  matches OBVR GameAddresses.h lines 1620-1626, "0 is high ... `add eax,68h`", and this function's
  `LEA ECX,[EBP+0x68]` at 0x00677EC6 / 0x00678342; the caller's ECX itself not read here - unverified).
- Return: none used (EAX is not set at the end; the FPU stack is empty after 0x00677500) (derived).

## Corrections after the refutation round

- B22/C21 (core): exactly 0.0 from +0x1D8 goes to +0x1DC, not to +0x1D4; pseudo.c's `0.0 <= x` is right for
  every ordered value and differs from the instructions only on NaN. The earlier "disagreement at 0.0" was a
  misreading of `TEST AH,5; JP` (read, 0x006780C2-0x006780CD).
- C11: the stagger grows only for actors whose +0x1D8 result is strictly negative (derived from B22).
- B39/C14: a NaN arg1 is passed through to the extra-list actors unchanged; 3600.0 only for arg1 <= 0.0 (read,
  0x0067825F-0x00678268). C14 now lists every filter before the update.
- C12/C17/heap: the 0x00674820 call and the extra-0x23 allocations are conditional (B25/B26; BL = 1 and
  extra 0x23 present); the 0x00674820 cost is O(k x m), not O(n^2) of the high list.

Smaller callees worth their own slice: 0x00677500 (high-actor budget / demotion), 0x00674820 with
manager+0x60 (shared with the render's shadow step), 0x005F1330 and the process +0x18/+0x20 branch, the
trio 0x005F2530/0x005F25F0/0x005F2720, vfuncs +0x1D4/+0x1D8/+0x1DC.
