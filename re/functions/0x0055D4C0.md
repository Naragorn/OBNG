## Purpose

Not a frame-rate display, not a profiler, not the console's timing. It is a
`(float time, bool flag)` update override of a NiNode subclass that owns a
FaceGen animation object (proposed: `BSFaceGenNiNode`'s
`UpdateDownwardPass`, with `BSFaceGenAnimationData` behind `this` vtable
+0x9C). It reads the tick globals to get **the difference of the last two
TimeInfo updates' tick offsets** (the wall-clock milliseconds between them
only under the conditions of C9/C10), turns them into seconds with the constant at
0x00A30E40, and hands those seconds to the FaceGen animation object as its
time step - with no clamp and without `fGlobalTimeMultiplier`.

Order of work (read, addresses in Control flow):

1. If `this+0x18` bit 0 (NiAVObject app-culled, xOBSE/OBVR) is set: only the
   base update `0x0070A0D0(time, flag)` and return.
2. `anim = this->vfunc_9C()`; `anim->vfunc_98()` (result ignored here).
3. If the byte setting at 0x00B120C4 is on, `this+0x111` is 0 and
   `0.0 < time`: `0x0055CC60(this, -1)`.
4. Gate: `anim` must be non-null; if `flag` is false `anim->vfunc_90()` must
   be true; if `time` equals the stored `this+0x10C`, `anim->vfunc_90()` must
   be true. A failed gate ends in the base update only.
5. Store `time` to `this+0x10C`.
6. Advance the face animation: when `this+0x106` and `anim+0x1D5` are both
   set, `anim->vfunc_94(0)` then `r = anim->vfunc_DC(seconds)`; otherwise
   `r = anim->vfunc_8C()` (no time step).
7. Head rotation block, when `this+0x106`, `anim+0x1D5`, `this+0x108` are set
   and the byte setting at 0x00B148F4 is off: `B = 0x0054B5A0(anim; &C,
   seconds, parent)`, `A` initialised to identity and passed to
   `anim->vfunc_60(&A)` (0x0055D745; whether vfunc_60 reads or writes `A` is
   not read), then `parent+0x64 = (parent+0x64 * B) * A` with `A` as
   vfunc_60 left it, through two calls of 0x007100A0,
   `0x0047CA90(parent; 0.0f, 0, this)`, and `r2 = 0x0054C6B0(anim; seconds,
   parent)`; `r = r || r2`.
8. `this+0x30` (NiAVObject local rotation) = the 3x3 matrix returned by
   `this->vfunc_A4(&D)`.
9. When `r` is set and (`anim->vfunc_98()` or `0x00674860(g_actorProcessManager;
   this+0x114, 10)` or `this+0x114 == g_thePlayer` or `IsMenuMode()`):
   `0x0055D1B0(this; r)`.
10. The base update `0x0070A0D0(time, flag)`, then `anim+0x1D5 = 1`.

The clock is read, never written (read: the only accesses to
0x00B39DB0/0x00B39DB4 are the `MOV`/`SUB` loads at 0x0055D615/0x0055D61B,
0x0055D6D8/0x0055D6DE, 0x0055D790/0x0055D796).

## Calling convention and parameters

- `__thiscall`: `ECX` = `this`, copied to `EBP` at 0x0055D4C7 and used as
  `this` throughout; `ECX` is still `this` at the first `CALL EAX` 0x0055D4FA
  (read).
- Two stack arguments, `ret 8` at all three exits (0x0055D4EE, 0x0055D89A,
  0x0055D8B8; read), callee cleans:
  - `E+4` float `time`: `FLD float [ESP+0xA0]` 0x0055D4D6 (ESP = E-0x9C),
    `FCOMP float [ESP+0xA0]` 0x0055D51C, `FLD float [ESP+0xA4]` 0x0055D57B /
    0x0055D5C8 / 0x0055D86B / 0x0055D89D (ESP = E-0xA0) (read).
  - `E+8` dword `flag`, used as a bool: loaded whole into `EBX` at 0x0055D543,
    tested as `BL` at 0x0055D550, passed whole to the base at 0x0055D4DD,
    0x0055D872, 0x0055D8A4 (read).
- Saves `EBP`, `EBX` (0x0055D53C..0x0055D8B0), `ESI`/`EDI`
  (0x0055D675..0x0055D80A) (read).
- Return value: none used by this body; `EAX` at the exits is the base call's
  result (two early exits) or `anim` (0x0055D889, normal exit). Ghidra types it
  void (unverified; no caller in the export to check).
- No direct callers (meta.json `"callers": []`, read): consistent with a
  virtual reached through a vtable (unverified which vtable, see Open
  questions).

### Ghidra pseudocode vs. instructions

The instructions win in each case:

- `FUN_0054c6b0(iVar5, piVar4)`: the instructions pass `(seconds, parent)`
  with ECX = `anim` (0x0055D79C..0x0055D7D3), not `(parent, &A)`.
- `FUN_007100a0(auStack_30, auStack_78)` / `FUN_007100a0(puVar11, puVar10)`:
  the instructions call with ECX = parent+0x64, args `(D, B)`, then ECX =
  result, args `(C, A)` (0x0055D74D..0x0055D76E).
- `FUN_0055d1b0(uVar13)` with `uVar13 = 0x55d66c` or `0`: the instructions
  pass the dword at `E-0x98` (C20).
- `FUN_0070a0d0(uStack_8, uStack_4)` at the normal exit: the instructions
  pass `time` and `flag` (0x0055D864..0x0055D879).
- `(**(code **)(*param_1 + 0x9c))(puVar10, fVar12, iVar5)`: those three are
  the arguments of the following 0x0054B5A0, not of the vfunc (C13).
- `param_1[0x43] = (int)param_2`: the instructions copy the float bits
  (`FLD`/`FSTP float` 0x0055D5C8/0x0055D5CF), no int conversion.
- `fVar12 * (float)_kMillisecondsToSeconds`: the instructions multiply by the
  double (`FMUL double` 0x0055D635) and round to float once afterwards; the
  constant is not narrowed first.

## Open questions

1. Which vtable holds 0x0055D4C0, and at which slot? Cheapest step: search
   the image for the dword `C0 D4 55 00` and read the RTTI complete-object
   locator before that vtable. Settles C4 and C5.
2. The names of the two settings at 0x00B120C4 and 0x00B148F4: read the
   strings at 0x00A64904 and 0x00A6EECC (data export of those addresses).
3. Do the tick globals 0x00B39DB0/4 have readers besides this body? Their
   address next to the FaceGen NiRTTI objects is no evidence of FaceGen
   ownership (C4: they occupy an 8-byte NiRTTI slot). An xref listing of
   0x00B39DB0/4 over the whole image settles it.
4. Does the main loop's TimeInfo update interleave with the LoadingMenu's
   private one within a frame (C10)? If yes, the face animation gets a wrong
   step during loading screens (for a negative int32 delta about
   2.15e6..4.29e6 s).
5. Which x87 precision control is active here (C7)? At PC64 the result is
   exact (`u * k` needs at most 56 bits, no double rounding); at PC24 negative
   deltas differ from `(float)((double)u * k)`; at PC53 exactness is unproven
   for `u >= 2^29` (same question as knowledge/frame-clock.md "x87
   precision").
6. What do `this+0x106`, `+0x108`, `+0x111` and `anim+0x1D5` mean, and who
   writes them? Only reads (and the one `anim+0x1D5 = 1`) are in this body.
7. What does `this->vfunc_A4` return - is the local rotation of the FaceGen
   node recomputed from the animation object each update?
8. For OBVR (do not edit OBVR from here): when C4 and C15 hold, the FaceGen
   node overwrites its parent's world rotation (presumably the head bone)
   during its own update pass, after the parent's world transform was
   computed. Head or neck rotations OBVR applies to that parent before this
   pass would be multiplied, not replaced, by `B * A`; anything applied after
   would be lost on the next pass. Unverified until question 1 and the parent
   identity are settled.
