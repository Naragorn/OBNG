# 0x0077A1F0 - NiD3DShader slot 14 (proposed: NiD3DShader::SetupShaderPrograms)

Slice: scene-pass (map only). Binary: Oblivion.exe 1.2.0.416, md5
`cdd2f0c5eff198d4f26b7b5b54ce4930` (the copy under
`~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/Oblivion/`; its 277 body bytes at
0x0077A1F0 equal the export's `bytes.bin`, checked byte for byte). Evidence
outside the export (vtable scan, the caller's and two virtual targets' `ret`
instructions) was read from that file with a pointer scan and `objdump`; no
REA, Ghidra or MCP tool was used.

## Purpose

Not a loop and not a walk. It is virtual slot 14 (+0x38) of `NiD3DShader`
(vtable 0x00A8977C) and is inherited unchanged by 30 of the game's shader
classes. The draws call it per skin partition in the skinned draw 0x00767520
(C6, read) and once per shader pass of the draw in the plain draw 0x007672F0
(C7, read with objdump: the call sits inside the pass loop that ends in
`JNE 0x767380` at 0x007674D2; once per draw only for single-pass shaders),
right after the shader's slot 13 (+0x34, e.g.
`ShadowLightShader_SetupTransformations` 0x007C9230) and slot 15 (+0x3C). Its
one direct caller is 0x0080FC20, SpeedTreeBranchShader's slot 14 (meta.json
callers; C18). In order it:

1. resets two fields of the `__NDL_SCMData` extra data on the first argument
   (an `NiObjectNET` - the geometry), looked up by name through
   `NiObjectNET::GetExtraData` 0x006FF9C0;
2. forwards all eight arguments plus the current pass index (`this+0x34`) to
   slot 1 (+0x04) of the current pass object at `this+0x3C` and keeps its result
   as the return value;
3. when present, applies two constant-map objects (`this+0x2C`, `this+0x30`)
   through their slot 16 (+0x40) against two program objects of the pass
   (`pass+0x44`, `pass+0x58`);
4. when the pass index is 0 and `this+0x24` is set, hands the result of
   `this+0x24`'s slot 29 (+0x74, argument 0) to slot 42 (+0xA8) of the object at
   `this+0x18`.

Its share of the sampler (49 %) is the inclusive time of the five virtual
calls V1-V5, the direct call D1 to `NiObjectNET::GetExtraData` 0x006FF9C0 and
its own small straight-line body (derived from C4 and C5).

## Calling convention and parameters

- `__thiscall`: `this` in ECX, copied to ESI at 0x0077A1FC and kept there to the
  end (read).
- Eight 4-byte stack arguments; the callee cleans them with `ret 0x20` at
  0x0077A302 (read).
- Returns in EAX the result of the pass call at 0x0077A247, saved in the
  argument-1 slot `[ESP+0x14]` at 0x0077A24E and reloaded at 0x0077A2FA (read).
- Callee-saved registers pushed: EBX, EBP, ESI, EDI (0x0077A1F0-0x0077A1F3, read).

## Claims

- **C1 (read)** 0x0077A1F0 is slot 14 (+0x38) of the vtable at 0x00A8977C, whose
  COL (0x00ACCB40) names the type descriptor 0x00B29470 `.?AVNiD3DShader@@`.
  xOBSE names 0x00B29470 `RTTI_NiD3DShader` (`re/symbols/xobse.csv:1307`,
  `obse/obse/GameRTTI_1_2_416.inl:363`). Two sources agree on the class.
- **C2 (read)** The same pointer sits at slot 14 of 31 vtables in `.rdata`:
  NiD3DDefaultShader, NiD3DShader, MapShader, BlurShader, MenuBGShader, SkyShader,
  HDRShader, ShadowLightShader, WaterShader, WaterShaderDisplacement,
  WaterShaderHeightMap, ParticleShader, TallGrassShader, BlurShader_P20,
  GethitShader, PrecipitationShader, SpeedTreeLeafShader, BoltShader,
  NighteyeShader, DebugShader, Lighting30Shader, RefractionShader, BSShader,
  BSImageSpaceShader, CopyShader, GeometryDecalShader, ParallaxShader, SkinShader,
  HairShader, SpeedTreeFrondShader, DistantLODShader. SpeedTreeBranchShader
  (vtable 0x00A9459C) has 0x0080FC20 at slot 14 instead.
- **C3 (unverified)** The method is Gamebryo's `NiD3DShader::SetupShaderPrograms`.
  No settled source names slot 14 (`re/symbols/xobse.csv`, `re/symbols/obvr.csv`,
  `knowledge/*.md`, `re/symbols/slice-*.csv`); 0x00767520.md (line 266) calls it
  "the constant uploads, unverified". The name is a proposal. Supported only by
  consistency: slot 13 is already proposed as
  `SetupTransformations` (`re/symbols/slice-draw-setup.csv`), the arguments are
  forwarded unchanged to the pass and constant maps, and the binary carries the
  RTTI names `.?AVNiSCMExtraData@@`, `.?AVNiD3DSCM_Vertex@@`,
  `.?AVNiD3DSCM_Pixel@@`, `.?AVNiD3DShaderConstantMap@@`, `.?AVNiD3DPass@@`
  (read: string search in the binary). The Gamebryo source is not public; I
  could not verify the name against it.
- **C4 (read)** There is no loop: the body has eight forward conditional
  branches and no backward branch (asm.s). The seed's guess "a loop over the
  visible set" does not hold.
- **C5 (derived from C4)** The 49 % on-stack share is the inclusive time of the
  calls V1-V5 and D1, not of iteration in this frame. Which of them carries the
  time is not measured (unverified).
- **C6 (read, in 0x00767520's export)** The skinned draw 0x00767520 calls, on the
  shader object EBX, slot 13 (+0x34) at 0x0076769F with 8 arguments, then slot 15
  (+0x3C) at 0x007676B3 with 4 arguments, and only when slot 15 returns non-zero
  (`JZ 0x00767804` at 0x007676B9) slot 14 (+0x38) at 0x007676E4 with 8
  arguments, the fourth being slot 15's return. The block starts at 0x00767650
  after alignment padding (`LEA ECX,[ECX]` at 0x0076764D), which suggests a loop
  head over partitions (unverified here; 0x00767520's own map owns the loop).
- **C7 (read, objdump of 0x007672F0 in the binary)** The plain draw 0x007672F0
  calls slot 13 (+0x34) at 0x007673C4, slot 15 (+0x3C) at 0x007673D5 and slot 14
  (+0x38) at 0x007673FB, the same order as the pseudocode
  (`re/export/rea-all/functions/0x7672f0.c` lines 56-60). The slot 13 call
  carries 0 as its pass argument (`PUSH 0x0` at 0x007673BE). The slot 14 call
  sits inside a pass loop: slot 19 (+0x4C) is loaded at 0x007674C9 and
  `JNE 0x767380` at 0x007674D2 jumps back to the loop head, so slot 14 runs once
  per shader pass with `this+0x34` as the pass index (consistent with B7, which
  treats the pass index as variable).
- **C8 (unverified)** That this frame (49 %) is on the stack more often than
  0x00767520 (40 %) (knowledge/skinned-vertex-streams.md:42) shows it is reached
  from more than the skinned draw. This holds only if the sampler counts a frame
  once per sample, both numbers come from the same run and sampler, AND the
  sampler unwinds completely through frame-pointer-omitted code; 0x0077A1F0 and
  its callers use EBP as a general register, so lost deeper frames alone could
  produce the gap. Statically the claim is consistent: 0x0080FC20 is a direct
  caller (meta.json, C18) and 0x007672F0 a virtual caller (C7). Settle with a
  caller-split of the samples (return address 0x007676E6 vs others), a call
  counter on slot 14 per call site, or by reading the sampler's counting rule.
- **C9 (read)** Slot 13 (0x007C9230 or any other) is not called here, and
  0x007693E0 is not dispatched from here: the only direct call is 0x006FF9C0, and
  the five indirect calls go through `this+0x3C`, `this+0x2C`, `this+0x30`,
  `this+0x24`, `this+0x18` - none through `this`'s own vtable.
- **C10 (read + xOBSE)** 0x006FF9C0 is `NiObjectNET::GetExtraData`
  (`re/symbols/xobse.csv:459-460`, `obse/obse/NiNodes.h:454/506`). Its argument
  here is the pointer stored at 0x00B29F84, which data.json reads as 0x00A8A9BC
  -> `"__NDL_SCMData"`.
- **C11 (unverified - pseudocode only)** 0x006FF9C0 enters the critical section
  at 0x00B3F600, binary-searches a name-sorted array (`ECX+0x10`, count as a word
  at `ECX+0x14`) with a byte-wise string compare, and leaves the section
  (`re/export/rea-all/functions/0x6ff9c0.c`). If that holds, every slot-14 call
  with a non-null geometry takes a lock and does a string binary search. This is
  the "lock" OBVR's table lists as 0x006FF9C0 in
  knowledge/skinned-vertex-streams.md (line 43): the address is the extra-data
  lookup, which locks - **a correction for OBVR**: the 4 % "locks (0x006FF9C0)"
  row names the lookup, not a lock routine.
- **C12 (read)** When the extra data is found, its words at +0x14 and +0x18 are
  set to 0 (0x0077A211, 0x0077A214). That `"__NDL_SCMData"` keys an
  `NiSCMExtraData` and that the two words are per-draw cursors of shader constant
  map entries is unverified (only the RTTI name exists, C3).
- **C13 (read)** V1's return value is this function's return value (stored
  0x0077A24E, reloaded 0x0077A2FA); V2-V5's return values are discarded except
  V4's, which is V5's argument.
- **C14 (read)** V2 and V3 receive the constant 1 as their 11th argument
  (`PUSH 0x1` at 0x0077A267 and 0x0077A2AA) and `this+0x34` as their 10th.
- **C15 (read)** `this+0x3C` is dereferenced without a null test (0x0077A225); a
  null current pass would fault. Precondition for the rebuild, not a flow.
- **C16 (read)** `this+0x18` is dereferenced without a null test when B7 and B8
  fall through (0x0077A2E5).
- **C17 (read)** The function touches no global besides 0x00B29F84; it reads and
  writes no clock or timer state (data_refs in meta.json lists only 0x00B29F84).
- **C18 (read, objdump of 0x0080FC20)** SpeedTreeBranchShader's slot 14
  0x0080FC20 calls 0x007B4290(1) at 0x0080FC28 (cdecl: the caller removes the
  argument with `ADD ESP,4` at 0x0080FC38), takes `[result+4]` and calls slot 16 (+0x40) of that
  object's `+0x2C` and `+0x30` against `[this+0x3C]+0x44` / `+0x58` with no null
  tests, then calls 0x0077A1F0 with ECX = this and its own eight arguments
  (0x0080FCCA) and returns 0x0077A1F0's EAX unchanged (`ret 0x20` at 0x0080FCD3
  with no EAX write after the call).
- **C19 (read)** Pseudo.c disagrees with the instructions for V2 and V3: it lists
  `(iVar1, param_2, uVar4, uVar5, uVar3, unaff_EDI, unaff_ESI, param_8, param_9, ...)`.
  The pushes at 0x0077A267-0x0077A28D give `(pass+0x44, arg1, arg2, arg3, arg4,
  arg5, arg6, arg7, arg8, this+0x34, 1)`. The instructions win. The pseudocode of
  0x0080FC20 (rea-all) declares it `void` and garbles the arguments of the call
  to 0x0077A1F0; the instructions (C18) win there too.

## Open questions

1. What slot 1 of the pass object (0x0075FBA0 for `NiD3DPass`, continuing at
   0x0075FCA0) does per draw - its pseudocode shows render-state calls through
   `DAT_00B42040` and slot +0x38 / +0x40 calls on `pass+0x44` / `+0x58` /
   `+0x48` (unverified). Is it where the shader programs are bound?
2. What 0x009A8E30 (slot 16 of `NiD3DShaderConstantMap` and both SCM subclasses,
   vtables 0x00AB2A4C / 0x00AB2BAC / 0x00AB297C) uploads, and whether the 1 in
   its last argument means "global" or "vertex".
3. Which of V1-V3 carries the 49 % (a sampler split by callee would settle it).
4. The meaning of the extra-data words +0x14 / +0x18 and who advances them
   (search for writers of `NiSCMExtraData`+0x14 in 0x009A8E30's callees).
5. The classes behind `this+0x18` and `this+0x24`, and what slots 29 and 42 are
   (a declaration handed to a render-state setter is a guess, unverified).
6. Whether other callers of slot 14 exist besides 0x00767520, 0x007672F0 (C7)
   and 0x0080FC20 (an xref search on `[vtbl+0x38]` over
   the shader hierarchy).
7. 0x007B4290(1) in 0x0080FC20: what global object it returns (the shadow scene
   object getter is 0x007B4280 per the manifest - a neighbour, unverified).

Callees worth their own slice, by expected weight: 0x0075FBA0/0x0075FCA0 (pass
apply), 0x009A8E30 (constant-map apply), 0x006FF9C0 (extra-data lookup with a
critical section, per draw), 0x0077A310 (NiD3DShader slot 15, gate of slot 14
in the draws), 0x00779710 (NiD3DShader slot 13).
