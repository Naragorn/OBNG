## Purpose

For each of three slots (loop 0x007C9160..0x007C921C, bound 0x00B4504C):
if the record the slot points to has a non-zero byte at +0x08, take the
three floats of a light position from the table at 0x00B46528, transform
them as a point by the matrix the caller passes (D3DXVec3TransformCoord),
take a fourth float from the table at 0x00B465A8, optionally adjust either
the transformed point (multiply by a scale) or the fourth float (divide by
the same scale), and store the four floats as one vec4 into the constant
block at 0x00B44FD8. A slot whose byte is zero leaves its vec4 unchanged.

Subsystems in order: (1) the slot records at [0x00B45040+4i] (a byte
flag only), (2) the light table 0x00B46528/0x00B465A8, (3) D3DX math,
(4) the constant block 0x00B44FD8. No D3D device call, no allocation, no
clock.

Correction for OBVR's wording ("transforms three light slots at
0x00B45040"): the records at 0x00B45040 are only tested at +0x08; the
vectors come from 0x00B46528 and 0x00B465A8 (read, 0x007C9160-0x007C9166 vs
0x007C916C/0x007C917B/0x007C918A/0x007C919C). OBVR may want to say "three
slots gated by 0x00B45040, positions from 0x00B46528".

## Calling convention and parameters

`__stdcall`, four dword arguments, callee pops 16 bytes (`RET 0x10` at
0x007C9229, read). No register input: ECX is first written at 0x007C9177
and EDX at 0x007C9186 before any read of them, EAX at 0x007C9160 (read), so
there is no `this` (derived from those three addresses).

Frame (read, 0x007C9140-0x007C914E): `SUB ESP,0x18`, then PUSH EBX, EBP,
ESI, EDI. Let B be ESP after the four pushes (entry ESP - 0x28). Locals:
B+0x10..B+0x18 the transformed point (out), B+0x1C..B+0x24 the input point.
Arguments: B+0x2C arg1, B+0x30 arg2, B+0x34 arg3, B+0x38 arg4 (derived
from the push arithmetic: `MOV EBX,[ESP+0x2C]` at 0x007C9144 executes after
one push, = entry+0x10 = arg4; `MOV EBP,[ESP+0x24]` at 0x007C9149 after two
pushes, = entry+0x04 = arg1).

| # | Stack | Type (proposal) | Use | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | entry+0x04 | `const D3DXMATRIX*` | held in EBP for the whole loop, pushed as pM of D3DXVec3TransformCoord | read 0x007C9149, 0x007C9172 |
| 2 | entry+0x08 | `float` scale | in B4 read once by `FLD float ptr [ESP+0x30]` at 0x007C91BA and kept on the x87 stack; the multiplies are 0x007C91C0 (`FMULP ST2`, x), 0x007C91CC (`FMUL ST1`, y, register operand) and 0x007C91D2 (`FMUL float ptr [ESP+0x18]`, z, with arg2 in ST0); in B5 it divides w (`FDIV float ptr [ESP+0x30]` at 0x007C91E0) | read |
| 3 | entry+0x0C | `int` pass/shader id (meaning unverified) | `EAX = arg3 - 0x177; CMP EAX,2; JA` | read 0x007C91A8-0x007C91B4 |
| 4 | entry+0x10 | `int` or pointer, used only as zero / non-zero | `TEST EBX,EBX` | read 0x007C9144, 0x007C919A |

The arg4 stack slot is reused as the w temporary: `FSTP [ESP+0x38]` at
0x007C91A2 overwrites it after EBX already holds the original value (read).
The function's own argument area is the callee's under stdcall, so a
rebuild need not reproduce that write (derived).

D3DXVec3TransformCoord is called with pOut = B+0x10 (EDX, 0x007C9186),
pV = B+0x1C (ECX, 0x007C9177), pM = arg1 (EBP, 0x007C9172), pushed right to
left (read). After the call nothing adjusts ESP and the epilogue at
0x007C9222 pops EDI directly, so the callee popped its 12 bytes (derived
from 0x007C9195..0x007C9229 having no ESP adjustment before the pops, and
from the [ESP+0x34]/[ESP+0x30] operands after the call addressing arg3/arg2
only when ESP = B again). Its return value (EAX) is ignored (read: EAX is
next written at 0x007C91A8 without being read).

Return: `void` (derived: no caller-visible result is computed into EAX on
purpose). EAX at `RET` is whatever was last written: D3DXVec3TransformCoord's
return, the arg3 arithmetic (0x007C91A8/0x007C91AC), or the slotRecord[2]
pointer from `MOV EAX,[EDI]` at 0x007C9160 when slot 2 is disabled (read,
those three writes are the only writes of EAX in the body).

## Control flow

Each row is one flow a rebuild test should exercise.

| # | Branch | Condition | Address | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | loop entry | unconditional `JMP 0x007C9160` | 0x007C9156 | enters the body with i = 0 (read) |
| B2 | slot gate | `byte [slotRecord[i]+8] == 0` -> `JZ 0x007C9210` | 0x007C9162/0x007C9166 | skips the transform and all four stores; vec4 i keeps its previous contents (read) |
| B3 | adjust gate | `arg4 != 0` -> `JNZ 0x007C91E8` | 0x007C919A/0x007C91A6 | stores the transformed xyz and lightW[i] unchanged (read) |
| B4 | id range | `arg4 == 0` and `(unsigned)(arg3 - 0x177) <= 2`, i.e. arg3 in {0x177, 0x178, 0x179} = {375, 376, 377}; `JA 0x007C91DC` not taken | 0x007C91AC-0x007C91B4 | x, y, z each multiplied by arg2 (0x007C91B6-0x007C91D6), w unchanged, `JMP 0x007C91E8` 0x007C91DA (read) |
| B5 | id outside range | `arg4 == 0` and arg3 < 0x177 or arg3 > 0x179 (unsigned compare, so negative arg3 also lands here) | 0x007C91B4 taken | w = lightW[i] / arg2 (0x007C91DC-0x007C91E4), xyz unchanged (read) |
| B6 | loop back | `EDI < 0x00B4504C` (signed `JL`) | 0x007C9216/0x007C921C | three iterations, i = 0, 1, 2 (derived: (0x00B4504C - 0x00B45040) / 4 = 3) |

Flow notes:
- The flags tested by `JNZ` at 0x007C91A6 come from `TEST EBX,EBX` at
  0x007C919A; the `FLD`/`FSTP` between them do not change EFLAGS (derived
  from the x87 load/store instructions not writing EFLAGS, Intel SDM; not
  re-checked in this run).
- The slot pointer is dereferenced without a null test (read, 0x007C9160 to
  0x007C9162): a null slot record faults (derived).
- arg4 and arg3 are loop-invariant but tested inside the loop per enabled
  slot (read); the outcome per slot depends only on B2 and on those two
  arguments (derived).
- Division by arg2 == 0 in B5 produces an x87 infinity or NaN unless the
  zero-divide exception is unmasked; the control word is not touched here
  (derived from no FLDCW in the body; the game's masking is unverified).
- Multiplication order in B4: x' = x*s (FMULP ST2), y' = y*s, z' = s*z
  (read 0x007C91C0, 0x007C91CC, 0x007C91D2). IEEE multiplication is
  commutative, so operand order does not change the result (derived). Each
  result is rounded to single by `FSTP float` (read).

## Claims

1. **read** (0x007C9229): `RET 0x10`, four stack arguments, callee clean-up.
2. **read** (0x007C9172-0x007C9195): the transform is `D3DXVec3TransformCoord(&out, &in, arg1)` with in = (lightPos[i].x, .y, .z) copied through single-precision stores to the stack.
3. **unverified** (D3DX documentation, not fetched in this run; the export has no D3DX body): D3DXVec3TransformCoord treats the input as (x, y, z, 1), multiplies by the matrix and divides by the resulting w. The D3DX documentation (or the D3DX9_27 body) would settle it.
4. **read** (0x007C91E8-0x007C920A): every enabled slot writes all four floats of constLightPos[i] at 0x00B44FD8 + 0x10i.
5. **read** (0x007C9166): a disabled slot writes nothing; the previous vec4 stays.
6. **derived** (from 2, B3-B5): the xyz output depends on lightPos[i], *arg1 and, only when arg4 == 0 and arg3 in 375..377, on arg2. The w output depends on lightW[i] and, only when arg4 == 0 and arg3 outside 375..377, on arg2. w never depends on the matrix.
7. **derived** (from 6 and the meta.json data refs, which list no other global): answer to the slice question - for the body's own reads, the output is a function of (slot flags, lightPos[0..2], lightW[0..2], the 64 bytes of the matrix at arg1 - its contents, not the pointer -, arg2, whether arg3 is in 0x177..0x179, arg4 != 0) plus the previous output for disabled slots. A cache keyed on those values is exact only under three preconditions: (a) the x87 control word (rounding, precision, exception masks) and D3DX's FPU/SSE state are the same between calls - the body has no FLDCW (read, asm.s), so the caller's control word is an input; (b) D3DXVec3TransformCoord (0x009A1B0A, D3DX9_27) is pure and reads only pV and pM - **unverified**, its body is outside the export and the D3DX documentation was not fetched; (c) neither the matrix at arg1 nor the slot records alias the output block 0x00B44FD8..0x00B45007 - **unverified**, would be settled by the callers' instructions showing where arg1 points. Outside those conditions 'exact' does not hold.
8. **derived** (from 6): the only part independent of the draw's matrix is w; it is the same across draws when lightW is unchanged and either arg4 != 0 or arg3 is in 375..377, or when arg2 is also unchanged. The xyz part is per-draw whenever the matrix changes per draw.
9. **unverified** (0x007C9230 pseudocode): arg1 is the inverse of the drawn object's world matrix (D3DXMatrixInverse into `afStack_21c + 0x33`, then passed as arg1), so the output is the light positions in object space; arg2 is `param_7[0xc]` (the scale of an NiTransform-like record), arg3 is the global `DAT_00b42e90`, arg4 is the caller's `param_2`. This rests on 0x007C9230's pseudocode only; the caller's instructions around the call (the pushes before `CALL 0x007C9140`) would settle it. Conditional consequence, valid only if it holds: the xyz part changes with every object and no per-frame cache applies; a cache would pay only when the same object (same matrix) is drawn in several passes.
10. **unverified** (0x007F6FC0 pseudocode): that caller passes a matrix it fills through 0x007F5B80 right before the call, and `*(iVar11 + 0xB8)` as arg4. 0x007F7EE0's pseudocode shows the call with no arguments (Ghidra did not recover them there).
11. **read** (meta.json, asm.s): no clock read or write, no D3D device call, no heap call.
12. **derived** (from the operand at 0x007C9144 being a plain `MOV`): pseudo.c's `iVar1 = (int)param_4` with param_4 typed `float` is wrong - there is no float-to-int conversion; arg4 is an integer or pointer tested for zero. The instructions win.
13. **read** (meta.json `calling_convention: unknown`) vs claim 1: Ghidra recorded no convention; the `RET 0x10` and the absence of register inputs settle stdcall.

## Open questions

- What the records at 0x00B45040/44/48 are and what their byte +0x08 means. 0x007C9230's pseudocode tests the same byte on pointers at 0x00B4501C, 0x00B45024, 0x00B45030 and 0x00B45068 (unverified), which suggests a table of shader-constant descriptors with a "used by the current shader" byte. Needs that table's writer.
- What arg3 is (the global 0x00B42E90 per 0x007C9230's pseudocode, unverified) and what ids 375..377 (0x177..0x179) select; 0x007C9230 also tests 0x177..0x17A and 0x180..0x187 (unverified).
- What lightW (0x00B465A8 + 0x10i) is - a radius or attenuation divided by the object's scale when the scale is not baked into xyz would fit B4/B5, but this is unverified. Why 0x007C9230 reads 0x00B465A8 as a direction vector (pseudocode) needs the instructions.
- Who writes 0x00B46528/0x00B465A8 and how often (per frame, per pass, per object). Settles whether claim 8's invariance is usable.
- Who uploads 0x00B44FD8 to the device (SetVertexShaderConstantF source), and whether a disabled slot's stale vec4 is ever uploaded.
- Per-draw cost: OBVR measured 15 % of a scene pass (knowledge/bone-palettes.md). With three D3DX calls per draw at most, the cost may sit in the D3DX call itself or in the call count; not separated (unverified).
