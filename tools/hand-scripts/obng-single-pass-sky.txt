# Single-pass stereo: the sky. The scene render puts the sky's root at the
# camera every pass (re/functions/0x0040C830.md step 6); the single pass does
# that once, for eye 0, so the second eye saw the sky from beside its centre -
# as near as its meshes are (Nadi, Windows, 2026-11-05: "sky and clouds seem
# unconnected to the world"). [Route] StereoFollowers=1 draws such geometry
# for the second eye without the view shift. At the Talos Bridge the head
# looks up 25 degrees in three directions, in clear and in cloudy weather;
# OBVR's eye pictures at the marks; OBNG.log names the camera followers it
# saw ("Route: camera follower").
#   tools/shadow-run.sh --ini tools/hand-scripts/obng-measure-unrouted.ini tools/hand-scripts/obng-single-pass-sky.txt
#   tools/shadow-run.sh --ini tools/hand-scripts/obng-measure-unrouted-followers-off.ini tools/hand-scripts/obng-single-pass-sky.txt

ini Render SinglePassStereo=1
ini Debug EyeDump=1
ini Hands Enabled=1
console-at ready cow Tamriel 1 15
console-at ready SetNumericINISetting "fAutoVanityModeDelay:Camera" 100000
console-at placed player.setpos y 66400
console-at placed fw 38EEE
console-at clear-done fw 38EF0

expect HandScript: finished
reject HandScript: WARNING

wait 12
mark ready
wait 20
mark placed
wait 6
head pitch 25
head yaw 0
wait 3
mark clear-1
head yaw 90
wait 3
mark clear-2
head yaw 180
wait 3
mark clear-3
head yaw 0
mark clear-done
wait 8
mark cloudy-1
head yaw 90
wait 3
mark cloudy-2
wait 2
mark done
