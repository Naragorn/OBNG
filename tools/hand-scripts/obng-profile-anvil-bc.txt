# directions for two seconds apiece, then an eight-second walk east. The walk
# follows the head, so the body is turned east by the console first
# (player.setangle z 90) and "head yaw 0" walks east. The console eats the
# sticks for about 8 s after a console-at; the sweep fills that time.
#
# The capture (one per run, at most 262144 events, about 25 a frame at
# 75 Hz) opens about 20 s into the script and closes after 125 s, over the
# three spots. Loads sit between the spots; perf-phases.py reads only the
# phases between the marks.
