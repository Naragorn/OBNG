# player held (tcl, setrestrained, the vanity camera off), thirty seconds
# empty, eighteen Walkers placed round him, their AI off eight seconds

# OBVR's runner keeps it the oldest save (the player's Continue never lands
# in it).

# Run, from OBNG's folder (OBNG.dll deployed, nothing patched):
#   tools/shadow-run.sh --ini tools/hand-scripts/obng-measure-original.ini tools/hand-scripts/obng-make-walkers-save.txt
